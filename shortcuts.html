Keys are matched by their position on a US layout, which is also how VIA's keycodes are named, so they work on any keyboard layout. They are ignored while you type in a field or hold Cmd, Ctrl or Alt, and they reach the studio only while its window is in front. The turning keys glide: hold one to keep turning, and press two to move diagonally. Each key shows a short readout of what changed.

Other pairs suit a wheel just as well: KC_EQL / KC_MINS for truss depth, KC_QUOT / KC_SCLN for line weight, or KC_RGHT / KC_LEFT to turn the sphere by hand. A second VIA layer is a good home for the layer toggles, KC_1 to KC_0.

3 · via

Putting a key on the Micro

  1. Open usevia.app in Chrome or Edge (it needs WebHID, which Safari does not have) and connect the Creator Micro.
  2. Click the key or wheel you want to change. A wheel has two slots, clockwise and counter-clockwise.
  3. Pick the keycode from Basic. The shifted symbols (KC_EXLM !, KC_AT @, KC_HASH #, KC_DLR $, KC_COLN :, KC_DQUO ") are in the shifted row of the same keyboard.
  4. For a combination such as Shift R, use Special › Any and type LSFT(KC_R).
  5. VIA saves to the Micro as you go. Bring the studio's window to the front and press.
