Depth: each strut takes its colour from its distance to you, From nearest and To farthest, so struts change colour as they turn toward or away from you.

Grain colour

Blend sets how lines mix where they cross and with the background. Overlay, soft light and the dodge and burn modes barely show on pure white or black; try a custom background. Grain is a fixed noise layer over everything, scaled with the image so exports match the view. GIFs leave it out; SVGs keep the blend but not the grain.

Video quality

Reel is 1080×1920 with the sphere centred, ready for Instagram without rescaling. For the crispest video, raise Line weight to 1.5× or more. GIF is transparent unless the background is included; its soft edges are blended toward the background colour, so set it to match where the GIF will sit. MP4 always includes the background colour. A background gradient shows in PNG, SVG and MP4; a GIF uses the start colour.
